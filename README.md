# Compound

A daily ledger of promises made and kept.

**Open the app: https://justasudonoob.github.io/Compound/**

Works offline and installs on iPhone, iPad and desktop. Your data stays on your own device.

## What's inside

- **Today**: plan the day, tick tasks off, earn XP, close out the day
- **Calendar**: recurring chores (daily, chosen days, or N times a week or month) and a month view where each day turns green (85%+), yellow (50 to 85%) or red (under 50%)
- **Learn**: level-by-level plans for Japanese, ML / Python, quant finance and fitness, a daily practice pick that adapts to what you actually do, and spaced reviews
- **Coach**: on-device learning that spots your patterns and flags tasks likely to slip, plus AI planning and chat three ways: free by copy and paste into Claude, ChatGPT or Gemini; free with a Google AI Studio (Gemini) key; or with your own Anthropic key. Keys stay on your device and are never exported
- **Board**: sticky-note promises, peeled off when kept
- **Goals**: deadlines on a live departures board, with steps and status
- **Growth**: your compound balance chart and level
- **Log** and **History**: a permanent record of every day
- A voice coach (female or male) that works offline

## Install on iPhone or iPad
